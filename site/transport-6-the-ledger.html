The ledger is the deliverable

What do you hand the person who has to sign off on this?

Not the number. The number plus the list of every place it crossed a boundary and what was assumed to get it across. That list is a Ledger, it is built by construction rather than by discipline, and check_complete refuses a ledger with a facet missing from it.

downgraded, not identified — and correctly so. Two of the three assumptions are still unverified: nothing here has checked the half-life against the window, or the Jensen gap against a nonlinear response. The status is a description of the evidence, not a grade. Verifying those two is what the calibrate and surface pillars are for.
