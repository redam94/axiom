The same library, {{examples.n}} different fields

A trial, a wheat field, a reactor, a nature reserve and a checkout page are the same mathematics wearing different words. That is the whole reason axiom's core has no domain in it — the nouns are treatment, dose, unit, outcome and covariate, and marketing is one adapter rather than the subject. These {{examples.n}} scripts are the argument for why that was worth doing.

Every one runs against a synthetic world whose truth is known, so the numbers can be checked rather than admired. The output shown below each one was captured by running it, not transcribed. Together they are {{examples.total_lines}} lines and take {{examples.total_seconds:.0f}} seconds.
